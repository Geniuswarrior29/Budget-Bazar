Budget Bazar

Privacy Policy - Nijata Neeti

1. Parichay (Introduction)

Budget Bazar ("hum", "hamara") Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh mein sthit ek local grocery aur daily essentials store hai. Yeh Privacy Policy batati hai ki hum aapki jaankari ko kaise collect, use aur protect karte hain jab aap hamari website ya app use karte hain.

2. Jo Jaankari Hum Collect Karte Hain

Hum sirf wahi jaankari collect karte hain jo aap khud provide karte hain:

  • Naam - jab aap review dete hain
  • Mobile number - review verification ke liye (1 number = 1 review)
  • WhatsApp messages - sirf order ke liye
  • Order details - delivery ke liye

Hum automatically koi personal data track nahi karte.

3. Jaankari Ka Upyog

Aapki jaankari ka upyog sirf in kamon ke liye hota hai:

  • Orders process karna aur delivery dena
  • Customer reviews verify karna (duplicate prevent karna)
  • WhatsApp pe order confirm karna
  • Aapke points/rewards track karna

4. Third Party Services

Hum kuch third-party services use karte hain:

  • Google Firebase - reviews aur orders store karne ke liye
  • Google Sheets - product catalog manage karne ke liye
  • ImgBB - product photos host karne ke liye
  • WhatsApp - order communication ke liye

Inki apni privacy policies hain jo unki websites pe available hain.

5. Data Sharing

Hum aapki personal jaankari kisi bhi third party ko sell ya share NAHI karte. Aapka data sirf hamare store operations ke liye use hota hai.

6. Data Security

Hum aapki jaankari ki suraksha ke liye reasonable measures lete hain. Firebase ke secure infrastructure pe data store hota hai. Lekin please dhyan rakhein ki internet pe 100% security guarantee nahi di ja sakti.

7. Children Privacy

Hamari app/website 13 saal se kam umra ke bachon ke liye nahi hai. Hum jaante-bujhte unka data collect nahi karte.

8. Aapke Adhikar (Your Rights)

Aap humse request kar sakte hain:

  • Apna data dekhne ke liye
  • Apna data delete karne ke liye
  • Koi bhi concern share karne ke liye

9. Cookies

Hamari website basic browser storage (localStorage) use karti hai app ki functionality ke liye jaise cart data aur user preferences. Yeh koi tracking cookies nahi hain.

10. Policy Mein Badlaav

Hum is policy ko kabhi bhi update kar sakte hain. Badlaav is page pe publish honge. Regular check karte rehein.
